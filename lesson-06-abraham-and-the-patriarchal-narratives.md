| Echo | What does it carry? |

<details><summary>Suggested answers</summary>

"Take": the command is stated plainly and heard as a test. · "your son...": the phrase tightens, naming the cost. · "Here I am": Abraham's readiness before God, then before his son. · "provide/see": the keyword the lesson reads as the theology of the story. · "rose early": prompt obedience, narrated without explanation. · the ram: the *substitutionary ram*, read in the lesson as foreshadowing God's provision.
</details>

## Level 4: Boss — The Misconception Buster 🧠 · 20 XP

The boss says:

> *"Abraham is presented as a model of unwavering, uncomplicated faith throughout Genesis."*

Defeat it in about 250 words. Your weapons are the lesson's five key takeaways:

- The Abrahamic covenant develops progressively across Genesis 12, 15, and 17, each encounter adding clarity.
- Genesis 15's covenant ceremony makes the promise unconditional, resting on God's faithfulness, not Abraham's.
- Abraham's faith is genuine but tested, including real doubt and failure along the way.
- The Akedah (Genesis 22) is the narrative and theological climax of the Abraham cycle.
- Abrahamic hospitality at Mamre models a theology of welcoming the stranger as potentially bearing divine presence.
