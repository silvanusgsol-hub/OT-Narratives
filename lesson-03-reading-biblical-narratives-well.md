## Level 4: Boss — The Misconception Buster 🧠 · 20 XP

The boss says:

> *"David and Goliath is a motivational story about facing your personal giants."*

Defeat it in about 250 words. Your weapons are the lesson's five key takeaways:

- Narrative criticism supplies a working vocabulary: narrator, plot, characterization, setting, dialogue, repetition, gaps.
- Literary analysis attends to genre, intertextuality, and rhetoric alongside narrative technique.
- Historical and Ancient Near Eastern context remain indispensable even for a literary reading.
- Canonical interpretation reads a text within the whole biblical story, not only as a self-contained unit.
- David and Goliath's real theological center is the LORD's sovereignty, not merely David's courage.
