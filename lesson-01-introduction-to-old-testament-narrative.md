## Level 4: Boss — The Misconception Buster 🧠 · 20 XP

The boss says:

> *"Narrative sections of the Bible are basically illustrations for the real theology, which lives in the epistles and the law."*

Defeat it in about 250 words. Your weapons are the lesson's five key takeaways:

- Narrative is a structured genre defined by sequentiality, characterization, point of view, gap-filling, and dialogue.
- Old Testament narrative does theology through story, not merely alongside it.
- Divine revelation works through the history behind the text, the artistry of the text, and the reader in front of it.
- No single interpretive method is sufficient alone, mature readers draw on several.
- How we read shapes how we live: narrative forms character and community, not just information.
