<details><summary>Guidance</summary>

## Level 4: Boss — The Misconception Buster 🧠 · 20 XP

The boss says:

> *"Joseph's rise to power in Egypt shows that God rewards good behavior with success."*

Defeat it in about 250 words. Your weapons are the lesson's five key takeaways:

- Jacob's election over Esau demonstrates grace working through, not around, human weakness.
- The wrestling at the Jabbok (Gen. 32) is the Jacob cycle's transformative climax, giving Israel its name.
- Genesis 50:20 states the Joseph narrative's core theology: divine providence working through human evil.
- Joseph's forgiveness of his brothers is hard-won, not immediate or cheap.
- Joseph's leadership integrates administrative competence with tested moral character.
