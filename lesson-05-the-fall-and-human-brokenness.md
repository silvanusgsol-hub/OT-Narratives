## Level 4: Boss — The Misconception Buster 🧠 · 20 XP

The boss says:

> *"The Flood story is primarily about a boat and animals, a children's story that adults should move past."*

Defeat it in about 250 words. Your weapons are the lesson's five key takeaways:

- Genesis 3 characterizes sin as cognitive, volitional, relational, and cosmic all at once.
- Cain's murder of Abel shows sin spreading from the individual into human community.
- The Flood narrative pairs genuine divine grief with an unconditional covenant commitment to preserve creation.
- Babel's judgment on human pride sets up, by contrast, the call of Abram that follows.
- In every episode of Genesis 3–11, divine grace accompanies divine judgment.

<details><summary>Lesson's ministry implication (check yours against it)</summary>

Genesis 3–11 gives pastors a theological vocabulary for naming both the reality of communal sin and the persistence of divine grace within it, without minimizing either.
