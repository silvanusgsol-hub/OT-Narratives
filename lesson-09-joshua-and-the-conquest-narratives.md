## Level 4: Boss — The Misconception Buster 🧠 · 20 XP

The boss says:

> *"The conquest narratives straightforwardly endorse religious violence as a model for believers today."*

Defeat it in about 250 words. Your weapons are the lesson's five key takeaways:

- Land in the Old Testament is a gift held in trust from God, not an object of absolute human ownership.
- Rahab's inclusion and Achan's judgment show the conquest narrative resists a simple ethnic reading.
- Herem is historically specific and framed as judgment on wickedness, not a timeless mandate for violence.
- No single hermeneutical approach fully resolves the ethical difficulty of the conquest narratives.
- The land-justice themes in Joshua speak directly to contemporary Adivasi and tribal land struggles.
