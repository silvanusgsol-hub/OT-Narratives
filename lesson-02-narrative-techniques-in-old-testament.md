## Level 4: Boss — The Misconception Buster 🧠 · 20 XP

The boss says:

> *"Repetition in biblical narrative is a sign of clumsy editing or multiple, poorly harmonized sources."*

Defeat it in about 250 words. Your weapons are the lesson's five key takeaways:

- Biblical narrators use an omniscient, reliable narrator whose evaluations carry real theological authority.
- Repetition, type-scenes, and Leitwörter are deliberate conventions, not stylistic accidents.
- Irony operates at verbal, dramatic, situational, and cosmic levels throughout the Old Testament.
- Narrative gaps are strategic silences that invite active reader engagement, not authorial oversights.
- Structural devices like chiasm and inclusio point interpreters toward a passage's thematic center.
