## Level 4: Boss — The Misconception Buster 🧠 · 20 XP

The boss says:

> *"The judges were straightforwardly heroic figures, like Sunday-school flannel-graph characters."*

Defeat it in about 250 words. Your weapons are the lesson's five key takeaways:

- Judges is structured around a repeating cycle: apostasy, oppression, outcry, deliverance, rest, relapse.
- None of the judges is a straightforwardly heroic figure; all are flawed, some morally compromised.
- The Spirit's empowerment of a judge does not guarantee that judge's moral integrity.
- Judges 17–21 shows the complete social collapse that results when 'everyone did what was right in his own eyes.'
- The book's refrain implicitly points toward Israel's need for faithful, covenant-shaped leadership.
