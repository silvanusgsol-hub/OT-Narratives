# Scoring

## Per lesson: 100 XP (+5 bonus)

| Level | Max XP | Track |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Term Cards | 15 | 🧠 Head |
| 2 Section Quests (any three, 10 XP each) | 30 | 🧠 ❤️ ✋ by quest |
| 3 Truth or Twist | 10 | 🧠 Head |
| 4 Boss: Misconception Buster | 20 | 🧠 Head |
| 5 Heart Quest (12) and Hands Quest (13) | 25 | ❤️ ✋ |
| ★ Signature Game | +5 | bonus |

Each extra Section Quest beyond three earns 5 bonus XP.

## Levels

A level is reached at 80% of the points available so far, so one missed quest never blocks progress.

| Level | After lesson | Points available | Unlocked at 80% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apprentice Scribe | 3 | 300 | 240 |
| Pilgrim Scribe | 7 | 700 | 560 |
| Wilderness Walker | 10 | 1,000 | 800 |
| Storykeeper | 13 | 1,300 | 1,040 |

To keep Head, Heart and Hands in balance, count the XP you earn in each track. Each of Heart (❤️) and Hands (✋) should account for at least 20% of your total before you claim a level.

## Lesson badges

A lesson badge is earned at 80 XP or more for that lesson. The badge names are listed in the README.

## Honest-play rules

1. Answer first, then open the `<details>` block.
2. Term Cards and Truth or Twist are self-scored. Be honest: the point is learning, not the number.
3. Heart quests are scored for completeness, never for the content of an opinion, prayer or doubt.
4. A tutor or study partner may sign off the Hands evidence.
